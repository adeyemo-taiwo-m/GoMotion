# Code Green Solar Commercial

## Goal

Create a premium 27-second vertical marketing commercial for Code Green, a serious solar-energy company operating in Nigeria. Move the viewer from a hot, frustrating power cut to comfort, confidence, and energy independence.

## Direction

- Format: 1080x1920, 30fps, vertical 9:16.
- Look: cinematic commercial, sophisticated contrast, realistic Nigerian residential and small-business cues, warm sunlight, deep green shadows, crisp equipment detail.
- Motion: smooth dolly pushes, controlled tracking, natural motion blur, intentional push-up transitions, no slideshow feeling.
- Palette: black, white and green from the official Code Green logo, with solar gold, charcoal, lithium blue and natural skin/wood tones.
- Typography: only the final end card uses typography; do not add random scene labels or claims.
- Sound direction: restrained tension in the outage, uplifting cinematic-electronic build after the solar reveal, environmental room tone and polished transition hits.

## Beats

- 0.0-5.2: Hot Nigerian home during a power cut. Fan slows, room darkens, owner is uncomfortable.
- 5.2-9.8: Rooftop solar installation in golden afternoon light. Modern panels, technician, realistic home.
- 9.8-15.2: Premium close-ups of panels, inverter and lithium battery system. Engineering and reliability.
- 15.2-20.7: Family and home continue comfortably with lights, fan and laptop operating.
- 20.7-27.2: Official Code Green logo, “Power Your Life. On Your Terms.”, “Reliable Solar Solutions for Homes and Businesses.”, “+234 812 103 0393”, “@codegreen_ltd”, “Make the Switch Today”.

## Acceptance criteria

- The official logo image is used unchanged and is not redrawn, stretched or recolored.
- No invented phone number, website, address, warranty, discount or performance claim.
- The contact phone number and Instagram handle are legible on the end card.
- All main visual elements stay inside the vertical safe area.
- The final end card holds long enough to read.
- The composition passes `givemotion check` with no errors or warnings.
